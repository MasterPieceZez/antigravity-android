Antigravity for Android is an unofficial standalone Android client for Google Antigravity (agy).

* Bundles a self-contained Linux environment under com.agy.os so it runs alongside stock Termux without root or PRoot.
* Launches the local Antigravity Hub in a fullscreen WebView with external browser OAuth handoff and native file uploads/downloads.
* Includes a built-in terminal overlay with extra keys, multi-account switching with Keystore-encrypted snapshots, and offline .tar.zst backup/restore.
